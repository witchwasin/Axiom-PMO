Logo concept

The product is not a calendar. It is a picture of the floor right now: one room taken, the rest free. Taken versus free is carried by fill against outline, not by hue, so the mark survives 24px, a monochrome print, and colour blindness.

What this product promises

Answer in one glance
Whether a room is free is readable without opening anything or asking anyone.
Book it yourself
No reception, no paper sheet, no waiting for someone to update a list.
Never double booked
An overlapping booking is refused, and the refusal names the booking in the way.
Colour means one thing
The accent colour appears only when a booking did not happen, so it never cries wolf.
