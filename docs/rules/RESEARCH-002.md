# RESEARCH-002 — Research artifacts contract

A project that declares `Research mode: guided` or `auto` must produce
`RESEARCH/RESEARCH.md` (status/scope, problem and questions, existing
solutions, feature parity, standards, differentiation, risks, Impact
Assessment, Change Proposals, explicit limits) and `RESEARCH/PROVENANCE.json`
(schema 1.0 with a claims array). Research off stays silent.
